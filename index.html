FAQs

Answer To Common Questions

Clear answers. Total confidence. Everything you need to know before you order.

How long does production take?

05-07 business days for normal production. For a rush fee we can make it within 02-03 business days and ship!

Can my graphics be etched into the stock chain?

No, we don't offer that on our stocks. All graphic work is a vinyl on top of the metal.

How long to make a fully custom premier chain?

After artwork is approved and the deposit is received (1/2 of the invoice to start production), normally 03-04 weeks. Not 12 weeks like other "companies."

How much do fully custom premier chains cost?

We can email you a price guide because there are lots of variations, but they range from $450 to $1300.

How much for shipping?

Shipping varies by destination, but it is low and insured. We are located in Ohio and generally ship UPS. For example, UPS Ground may only be $16.99 with insurance. We can also ship internationally with other carriers; just contact us for prices.
