Which Rob James FX product is right for me?

Matte Look™ — lightweight, everyday anti-shine scalp cream with daily hydration. Shine Block™ — maximum shine control for very shiny or oily scalps, events and photography. Matte Shield™ — tinted mineral SPF 40 sun protection for the scalp + face with a natural matte finish. Everyday. Maximum. Sun.

Does Matte Shield leave a white cast?

Like all mineral sunscreens, it may leave some visible cast at first. Its subtle tint helps the zinc oxide blend naturally, and the cast diminishes as it’s massaged in, leaving a natural matte finish. Final appearance varies with skin tone and amount applied.

Which Rob James FX product is right for me?

Each product does a different job: Matte Look is the daily anti-shine cream, Shine Block is the strongest dedicated anti-shine product, and Matte Shield combines mineral SPF 40 with a matte finish. They aren’t three versions of the same product — each is purpose-built for a specific scalp need.

Is Rob James FX specifically designed for bald and shaved heads?

Yes. It was developed around exposed scalp skin — bald heads, shaved heads and Scalp Micropigmentation (SMP). Founder Rob James has spent 10+ years working with shaved scalps and completed 3,000+ procedures.
